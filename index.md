# Nathanael Wayashe

Music Business student at Columbia College Chicago. I make experimental music under the name FÉRINA and I'm learning creative coding in Processing, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

![Screenshot of my work](screenshot.png)

## Projects

### Composition Creator
A Processing app that generates random abstract compositions using loops, conditionals, and functions.

### Confetti Burst
A Processing sketch built with classes and objects. Click anywhere to launch a burst of confetti.

### Drone Field
An experimental audio piece made with the Processing Sound library. Three detuned oscillators and noise create a drone you can play with the mouse.

### Guessing Game
A number guessing game built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
